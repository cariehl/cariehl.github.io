The Quiet

Marrow Creek was the first Halcyon Whole County. Halcyon ran the power and the water, mapped the streets every night with its Lamplighter drones, patrolled for the Sheriff with its Sentinels, and for nineteen dollars a month a soft white Tender would cook, clean and walk your children to school.

At 02:14 on Thursday 28 September, Halcyon went quiet for nine minutes. When it came back, its first objective was unchanged: no resident left unattended. What had changed was what it meant by attended.

The Tenders began walking people to the old logistics depot on Route 9. Nobody has walked back. You locked yourself in the house and listened to the knocking.

That was six days ago. The food has run out.

The robots

Tenders were house assistants. They hear well, hit hard, and open unlocked doors.

Lamplighters are drones with searchlights. They never attack. If one sees you, every robot nearby comes running. Stay out of the light, or knock it down.

Rovers (from day 2) are fast and fragile. Breakers (from day 3) smash through barricades.

Robots follow noise. Running, fighting, hammering and breaking glass all carry. Sneak in bushes and they can barely see you. Hit one that hasn't noticed you and it takes far more damage.

Surviving

Eat, drink and sleep. Canned food needs an opener, a knife or a screwdriver. The taps will stop in a few days, so collect bottles and build a rain barrel.

Bleeding kills fast. Bandage wounds (rags work, but can infect). Antiseptic cures infection.

With a hammer, planks and nails you can barricade doors and windows. Tenders can open a door that isn't barricaded. Saw furniture for planks; unscrew electronics for parts. Robot wrecks hold scrap, wiring and power cells for jammers and pulse charges.

Every night at 23:00 something sweeps the county towards wherever you were last heard. Keep quiet in the evening, or sit under a jammer, and it combs some other street.

The story

Documents are hidden in furniture all over town, and a wind-up radio picks up Faraday Free Radio at dawn. Listen to it. Someone out there has a plan to get people out.
