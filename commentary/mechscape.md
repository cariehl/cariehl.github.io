# Commentary on MechScape (Working Title)

I grew up playing a lot of RuneScape. I really loved that game (and still do), and even tried to apply for a job at Jagex, the company that created it. (I was declined on the basis of not living in the UK, their country of operation. I suspect this was a kind way of telling me "you are not qualified", which I clearly was not.)

At some point in 2006, Jagex released a big announcement about their next upcoming MMORPG, "MechScape". The hype was real! The forums were abuzz with speculation. We all wondered what RuneScape would look like if the world was carried hundreds of years into the future. Then the game was cancelled, then it was renamed to "Stellar Dawn", and then... nothing. They released Transformers Universe, it flopped (as far as I know), and everyone moved on with their lives. But I was still stuck on MechScape.

This game is a small slice of what I imagine MechScape could have looked like. It draws very obvious inspiration from RuneScape, down to the 0.6-second tick system. Combat is turn-based, skills are a grind, and quests are relatively short with punchy or quirky dialogue. Crafting is in, graphics are chunky, and of course, there are mechs.

This game has been taking up space in my head for the past 20-odd years at time of writing, so it is a relief to see a real implementation of it. The whole game, graphics, music, dialogue, gameplay, were all implemented by Claude Opus 5.5 via Claude Cowork. I did not touch a single line of code, and only made minor edits to quest dialogue and item/environment descriptions.

The most impressive part of this project was watching Claude take the initial Godot prototype and rewrite it in three.js in a matter of minutes. That moment made me realize what Claude is truly capable of in terms of development. "Build one to throw away" no longer feels like a pipe dream.

If you have suggestions for a better name before Jagex's lawyers track me down, @ me on GitHub. Enjoy.
